# Anti-Slop & Design Taste Rules for Antigravity AI

Refer to [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) for the complete 34-point specification.

## Summary of 34 Prohibited Flaws:
1. Purple gradients (`from-purple-600 to-indigo-600`)
2. AI slop photos / hyper-perfect stock imagery
3. Pill-shaped desktop buttons
4. Pure black `#000000` / pure white `#ffffff`
5. Unstyled text-only logos
6. Low-contrast hero text colors
7. Cursive / script fonts
8. Emojis as primary UI action icons
9. Intrusive / jittery scroll animations
10. Vague hero text ("Unlock your potential")
11. Fake metrics & fabricated stats
12. Fake visitor count widgets
13. Fake customer counts & review cards
14. Excessive em-dashes (—)
15. Lorem Ipsum text
16. Duplicate page titles across routes
17. Multiple H1s or missing H1 tag
18. Missing `<meta name="description">`
19. Missing `og:image` tags
20. Missing JSON-LD structured data
21. Missing `<link rel="canonical">`
22. Missing `llms.txt`
23. AI-blocked `robots.txt`
24. Missing favicon (causing 404 console logs)
25. Missing `sitemap.xml`
26. Missing `<html lang="en">`
27. Empty view-source SSR/static shell
28. Missing dedicated 404 page component
29. Missing image `alt` text
30. Browser console errors or unhandled warnings
31. Massive un-split JS bundles
32. Broken / dead button click handlers
33. Single-page superficial landing page masquerading as full app
34. Missing Privacy Policy (`/privacy`) and Terms (`/terms`) links
